3. Safeguards

A pharmacist verifies the current medication list before any packing starts.

Packed aids are checked by a second person before release.

Written consent is held for each patient whose packs are PPA-funded. (Marked to verify.)

Changes to a patient’s medicines are confirmed with the prescriber before the next pack.
