R.Pharmacy · lead designer’s brief · 18 November 2026

A calm, honest control room for a busy proprietor.

The proprietor opens R.Pharmacy for a minute between other jobs, often one-handed, sometimes at night. They need to know what needs them, why, and how to deal with it in a few taps. The app must never pretend things are fine when they have not been checked.

CalmQuiet screens. Colour only where it means something.
HonestNot checked is never shown as fine. No compliance scores.
FastThree taps from Today to the thing that needs doing.
AccountableWho did what, and when, is always one tap away.
Inspection-readyEvery check shows its source and when it was last done.

Rules every direction follows

  • Today opens on what needs you, in the order you act.
  • Status always has a word and a symbol, never colour alone.
  • The four check answers: Satisfactory, Issue, Needs verification, N/A.
  • An Issue asks one follow-up and offers to create a Task.
  • Legal deadlines look different from your own chosen reviews.
  • Touch targets at least 44 px, thumb-reachable answers.
  • One bottom navigation: Today, Audits, Tasks, More.
  • Fictional data only; no patient details anywhere.

Shared status language

● Overdue ◐ Needs verification ○ Not checked yet ◇ Coming up ✓ Done · verified § Legal requirement
