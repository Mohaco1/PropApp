# Suggestions (none of these are implemented)

## Critical
- **C-1 · S8 date limit in "Change due date".** Apply the same 3-month check the review form already uses (s 111, unless NSW Health has approved other times), so the S8 date can't be moved more than 3 months after the last stock check. This changes a compliance behaviour, so it needs your approval.
- **C-2 · Real-device UAT before relying on it.** Test on your phone and laptop, especially the full-screen editor and the Calendar on a small screen.

## Recommended
- **R-1 · "Why it's here" line** on Overview items (Concept D): one plain sentence per item type, for example "Due 14 Nov · legal basis s 111". It's display only, but the wording needs your sign-off.
- **R-2 · Year plan "Expected" marks**: show future repeats from each review's interval, clearly labelled as expected rather than scheduled.
- **R-3 · Onboarding presets** (screenshot 4), as tick-shortcuts only: for example "Common community pharmacy" (DAA, Home delivery, Vaccination, Medicines reviews). The list needs your confirmation; it's currently an estimate, like the service order.
- **R-4 · Month tab layout**: put the day list beside the month grid on a laptop, as in N10.
- **R-5 · Calendar filters**: area and status filters on the Weekly tab.

## Optional
- **O-1** J/K and 1–4 keyboard shortcuts (Concept D).
- **O-2** "All pharmacies" combined view, if you own more than one pharmacy (see Decisions D-1).
- **O-3** Print view of the Year plan.
- **O-4** A colour-blind-safe check of the red, amber and green words. The words already carry the meaning, so this is low priority.
