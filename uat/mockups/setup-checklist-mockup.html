Setup: a checklist on Today, and a clear start

Two small changes from the Shopify and Duolingo onboarding reviews. Fictional Harbourview Pharmacy with 4 services: DAA, OTP, Vaccination and Home delivery. Nothing in the app has changed yet.

1 · Today: show the steps left, not just a bar

Now, Today shows a bar and “2 of 6 steps”, so the owner can’t tell what’s left or how long it takes. Proposed: a short checklist of the remaining steps with the next one highlighted, and a clear “you’re set up” moment at the end.

2 · Setup: say up front what’s needed to start

Now, the first screen says “About 15–20 minutes” but not what the steps are or which can wait. Proposed: the steps listed before you begin, marked “Needed to start” or “Can wait”, and a thin progress bar at the top of every setup screen.

Not changing: the questions themselves, “That’s how we do it / Change / Check later”, the per-service “What happens next” screen, and the “Just bought?” option. This only adds a map at the start, a progress bar, and a checklist on Today.
