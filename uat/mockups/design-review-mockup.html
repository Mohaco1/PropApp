Design review: eight fixes, current vs proposed

In priority order. Fictional Harbourview Pharmacy. Nothing in the app has changed. Pick what you want built.

1+3 Calm Today2 One way into a visit4 One set of words5 Lighter action screen6 Messages at the bottom7 Bottom bar8 Polish

1 + 3 · A calm Today: one card on top, numbers shown once

Now, up to five cards sit above the first job and “3 overdue” appears four times. Proposed: one top card that shows only the most important thing (setup unfinished → visit in progress → backup old). The counts live on the four buttons, and the status line becomes the date plus one sentence.

2 · One way into a site visit

Now, “Start with these 3 → Proprietor site visit → Start” skips the prepare screen that the “Start a visit” card shows. Proposed: every route into a site visit opens the same prepare screen first.

4 · One set of words

Now: jobs, actions, tasks, reviews, checks and items, and four ways to say “not sure yet”. Proposed: three words for the work and one for “not sure yet”, used everywhere, including the screens that still say “Task”.

5 · A lighter action screen on a phone

Now, opening an action shows the whole form: a cut-off title box, “Responsible person or role” (always you), due date and status. Proposed: the title as text, the three things you actually do as buttons, and the form under “Edit details”.

6 · Messages at the bottom, not over the title

Now, “Saved proprietors” and similar messages cover the screen title. Proposed: they slide up just above the bottom bar and leave after 3 seconds.

7 · A bottom bar that matches an owner’s week

Now, Staff is hidden under More while Calendar takes a main spot. Proposed: Staff on the bar, Calendar under More (it’s still one tap away, and Today already shows what’s due). This one is your call from how you work.

8 · Smaller polish

Four small things that add up.

Not touched: the colours and type, the one-question-at-a-time site visit, the filter buttons, the setup list, and the Required by law / Recommended tags. Dates show day first (24/11/2026) on an Australian phone; the “Now” screenshots came from a browser set to US format.
