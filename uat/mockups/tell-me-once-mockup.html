A · Tell me once: DAA packing moves to an external packer

The owner records the change once. The app shows what's different and lists only the few things that depend on it. Each item says why, whether it's required by law or recommended, and where it comes from. The owner decides each one now or later. Nothing is adopted, closed or changed for them.

B · New buyer: confirm inherited facts while you’re there

Answers marked “Inherited · check later” get a useful home on the first visit. Only facts you can check on site are offered, three at a time. Correcting one that matters, like DAA packing, opens the Tell me once flow above.

Rules this would follow

  • One change event. It's saved once with its start date and the date you recorded it. Opening it again resumes it; it never makes a second draft or action.
  • Nothing is decided for you. Procedure changes stay drafts until you adopt them. Open actions stay open. Past reviews still describe how things were done then.
  • Law vs recommended is always shown, with the source in one tap. Recommended items can be left for later without turning red.
  • First version covers only DAA. A change of pharmacist in charge and starting or stopping a service come next, once this proves useful.

Before building: the external-packer rows reuse wording the app already cites from Regulation 2026 s 136–137 and NSW Health. I'd re-check that page and the Board guideline for any other duty that changes with outsourcing before this goes live.
