Setup and Today: two small changes

A mock-up with fictional data. Left is how the app looks now on a phone; right is the proposed version. Nothing in the app changes until you pick.

1 · Pharmacy setup as a settings list

Now it's a three-column table, and the columns squeeze on a phone. Proposed: one row per item, with an icon, a one-line summary, a clear status chip and a chevron, grouped the same way as today.

2 · Today filters as four segments

Now there are eight tabs in a strip that runs off the screen, so most filters are hidden. Proposed: the four you'll use most as segments with their counts, and "More filters" opens the rest from the bottom.

Also borrowed: the small grey section pill (e.g. “Law and inspections · S8”) could sit above page titles to show where you are. It isn't drawn here to keep the comparison simple. Nothing else from the Musais site is used: no dark background, gradients or glow.
