# UAT v32: S8 inventory limit (approved 19 Nov 2026)

**Link:** https://claude.ai/artifact/BKM3ccSc1uYD5A1T7mPXbk. It's new; v31 keeps its own link.
**Code:** `layers/s8-rule.js`. The v27 engine is unchanged.
**Rule:** Regulation 2026 s 111(1)(b). An S8 inventory is due every 3 months, unless NSW Health has approved other times.

## What changed
- **One guard on the app's save step.** Every way the S8 date can change goes through it:
  - completing the review
  - Change due date
  - Calendar (all tabs) and Plan in plain words
  - Set a first date, and Initial review schedules
  - reminder-only saves
  - the setup intervals

  It refuses a date later than **3 months after the latest recorded stock-check date**, using v27's own `legalLatest('B1')`. If no stock check is on record, the limit is 3 months from today. **If the inventory is already overdue, you can plan it any time in the next 7 days** (your decision: "this app is to help, not police"). The messages say what to do rather than what's wrong.
- **The S8 date can't be removed** while S8 is an active review.
- **Change due date window (S8 only):**
  - It shows the latest allowed date.
  - It has a tick box, "NSW Health has approved other inventory times", which needs a reference. The approval is saved with who and when, and appears in History.
  - The approval can be removed later, and then the limit applies again.
- **S8 review form:** entering the stock-check date sets the latest allowed next date (stock date + 3 months) and shows it. Before this, the form suggested today + 3 months, which could be one or more days too late.
- **Fixed alongside:** on review screens, the 1–4 key hints now restart for each question, and the keys answer the question you're on.
