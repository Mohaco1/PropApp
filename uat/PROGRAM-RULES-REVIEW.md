# Setup questions checked against the PPA program rules and the Regulation (20 Nov 2026)

Sources (supplied by the owner): PPA Program Rules for DAA (Sept 2026), Staged Supply (Sept 2026), HMR, RMMR, MedsCheck and Diabetes MedsCheck; NSW OTP Standards (Nov 2026); Medicines, Poisons and Therapeutic Goods Regulation 2026 (SL 2026-330).

## Verified and already in the app
- **DAA labels (Reg s 136):** date packed, patient date of birth, no oral and external-use medicines in one pack, third-party reference number; label records kept by the pharmacist and the packer (s 136(3)); records kept at least 2 years (s 185(3)); third-party manufacturing instructions follow the prescription (s 137). All match.
- **S8 by carrier (Reg s 191):** opaque covering, nothing outside showing S8, a contents list marked "SCHEDULE 8/9—CHECK CAREFULLY". Matches.
- **OTP Standards (community pharmacy items 1–7):** all seven items are covered by the OTP setup and its checklists.

## Added now (verified)
- DAA labels: Poisons Standard **Appendix L** information on the label (s 136(1)(a)). It was missing.
- S8 courier wording: the marking must be in **bold capitals at least 12.5 mm high**, and the package **properly addressed** (s 191(2)).

## Pharmacist prescribing (research, not yet changed)
- Current NSW scope reported: uncomplicated UTI, continuing hormonal contraception (initiation for women 18+ from 1 June 2026 per the Pharmacy Guild NSW branch), impetigo, shingles, atopic dermatitis, psoriasis. The app's list matches the first group.
- From 5 Nov 2026 a regulation recognises graduate-certificate pharmacists to manage further conditions (reported as six, including acne and hay fever); a trial for 23 chronic conditions is announced for early 2027.
- The official NSW Health list for the newer conditions was not found. Proposal: add "Contraception: start (women 18+)" and an "Expanded scope (graduate-certificate pharmacist)" option with "describe", and source-check before release.
