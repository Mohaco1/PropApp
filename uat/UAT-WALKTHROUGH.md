# UAT walkthrough: R.Pharmacy v28 · Concept D

Use **fictional data only**. Note anything odd in `UAT-PROGRESS.md` (or just tell me: page, what you expected, what happened, and a screenshot).

## 1. First use (about 10 min)
- [ ] Open the app in a fresh browser (or private window). The landing page shows "Set up your pharmacy".
- [ ] Enter a fictional pharmacy name, address and proprietor.
- [ ] **Services step:** the tiles, progress bar and "Your plan so far" update as you tick. Tick 3–4 services.
- [ ] Press **Save service selection**. Each service's setup questions follow, as before. Finish one, then "Save & Finish Later" on another.

## 2. Look and navigation (5 min)
- [ ] Laptop: tabs across the top; the Calendar tab sits after Audits.
- [ ] Phone: the bottom bar (Home, Audits, Tasks, More); Calendar is under More.
- [ ] Ctrl+K (⌘K on a Mac) or "Search or jump to…": type "History" and press Enter.
- [ ] "Recording as" in the header still changes the reviewer.
- [ ] Open any record. On a laptop it slides in from the right; on a phone it fills the screen. Esc or Close shuts it.

## 3. Calendar (15 min)
- [ ] **Weekly breakdown:** items sit in the right week, each appears once, and the words (Overdue, Due soon, Upcoming, In progress, Completed) look right.
- [ ] **Year plan:** Done, Overdue and Planned match what you've recorded; § is on legal or external dates; the previous and next year buttons work.
- [ ] Set 5 reviews into one month (through Audits). The "looks heavy" note appears, and "Change date" opens the usual dialog.
- [ ] **Month:** select days; "Schedule meeting on…" works.
- [ ] **Plan in plain words:** type "S8 check 1st Dec morning", then Find, then Add. The date dialog opens with 1 Dec filled in. Cancel and check nothing changed. Try again and save.
- [ ] Try pushing a date later: it must ask for a reason.

## 4. Staff (10 min)
- [ ] Add 2–3 fictional staff with different roles, one with a credential expiring soon and one expired.
- [ ] The matrix words look right (To verify, Expires…, Expired, Overdue).
- [ ] Laptop: click a row and the detail pane opens. Click the name and the full record opens.
- [ ] Phone: the issues show under the name.
- [ ] Record an annual assurance review and check the matrix updates.

## 5. Everything else still works (15 min)
- [ ] Overview: counts, filters and the detail panel.
- [ ] Audits: complete one review, including the next date.
- [ ] Tasks: add one, filter, complete it.
- [ ] Policies: open one procedure and export it (from a local copy, because downloads are blocked in the claude.ai viewer).
- [ ] Meetings: add one; duplicate the agenda.
- [ ] History: filters; export CSV (local copy).
- [ ] Services: configure one, then disable and re-enable it.
- [ ] Pharmacy setup: add a second fictional pharmacy. The header switcher appears; switch back and forth.
- [ ] Backup: download and restore (local copy).
