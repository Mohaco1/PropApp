# PRU officer reassessment (20 Nov 2026, after the NSW Health gap fixes)

This is my view of how a NSW Health Pharmaceutical Regulatory Unit officer might see the app during an inspection. It is not anything they've said.

## What they'd now rate well
- **S8 in one place.** "S8 at a glance" brings together:
  - the inventory date (s 111);
  - who the law puts it on: the proprietor for the register (s 108–111) and the pharmacist in charge for storage (s 122);
  - where each S8 record is kept and when it was last seen;
  - S8 answers from setup and open S8 actions;
  - NSW Health's own steps for missing or stolen S8, a lost register, destruction and forged prescriptions.
- **The S8 review now tests the mandatory Medicine Storage Standards:**
  - safe specification and fixing;
  - keys and codes;
  - register rules;
  - 24-hour receipts;
  - e-register governance.
  It is no longer "was the stock check done?".
- **Legal obligations by section.** Each line also carries the Standards and authorities an inspector would cite: Storage, OTP, Vaccination, Class Authorities and vaping.
- **Official checklists.** The vaping review is NSW Health's own self-assessment tool, line for line, the same document an officer would use.
- **Sources are honest.** Vaping and cannabis procedures cite NSW Health pages. Only TGA-only steps still say "verify", and nothing with a marker can be approved.
- **Key dates are tracked:**
  - prescribing authorities expire 4 Nov 2027;
  - vaccination Standards expire 4 Nov 2028;
  - eNRMC transition ends 31 Mar 2027.
- **Selling, buying or closing:** a list for change of ownership (OTP re-registration, new AIR number and VAN, s 111(3) inventory).

## What they'd still call inefficient or weak (ranked)

1. **The records live in one browser, without sign-in.**
   - An officer can't rely on records that can vanish or can't be tied to a person.
   - This is still the biggest gap, and the reason approval is switched off.
   - It needs the roadmap's hosting and sign-in step.
2. **There's no inspector pack.**
   - The S8 page and the Legal obligations list should export as one dated PDF, along with the last site visit, open legal actions and staff checks.
   - At the moment the officer would be shown screens.
3. **There's no log of notifications made.**
   - The app tells you to notify Pharmaceutical Services (S8 loss, forged script, lost register) and the TGA (SAS Category C, adverse events).
   - It doesn't record that you did: date, form and reference, with no patient details.
   - An officer would ask "when did you report it?".
4. **Per-person evidence sits inside reviews, not on the person.**
   - Several items belong to individual staff:
     - OTP Standards acknowledgement by every pharmacist;
     - immuniser declaration form;
     - cold-chain module certificate;
     - CPR within 12 months;
     - training per prescribing condition;
     - Working with Children Check.
   - These are ticked in reviews. They should be dated entries on each staff profile, with expiry reminders.
5. **Long review lists encourage ticking.**
   - The S8 review went from 3 checks to 9.
   - Grouping or "show me the evidence" prompts (e.g. "photo of the safe fixing, held locally") would keep it honest without making it longer.
6. **There's no risk rating on actions.** Pharmaceutical Services rates non-compliance as Low, Medium, High or Extreme. Open actions should carry that rating so high-risk ones come first.
7. **Open questions:**
   - The vaccination record rule about the GP is unresolved (P-6).
   - Some TGA, Pharmacy Board, NSP and certificate steps are still unverified.

## Recommended next three
1. **Notifications log:** what was reported, to whom, when and the reference, with no patient data.
2. **Inspector pack:** a one-tap dated PDF of the S8 page, legal obligations, last site visit, open legal actions and staff checks.
3. **Staff evidence:** dated per-person records for the items in point 4, with expiry reminders.

Sign-in and hosting stays the step that makes all of it count as evidence.
