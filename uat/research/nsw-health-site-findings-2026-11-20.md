# NSW Health Pharmaceutical Services site — findings (read 20 Nov 2026)
## p051 Legislation changes for pharmacists (updated 23 Oct 2026)
- Owner of the pharmacy responsible for keeping a compliant drug register on premises and ensuring staff comply; daily entries by pharmacist on duty doing the S8 activity.
- S8 balance recorded at least every 3 months (was March/Sept).
- Mandatory Standards from 5 Nov 2026: OTP Standards; Medicine Storage Standards (S4D + S8); Electronic Drug Register Standards; Vaccination Standards.
- Urgent-use supply: ARTG-registered only; written, signed orders by authorised person; records kept 2 years. RCF: approved by medicines governance group, signed order of DON/nominated manager. First aiders: listed meds, current first aid certificate.
- Wholesale supply from community pharmacy allowed in listed cases (ownership change; another pharmacist for individual patient; same-ownership pharmacies excl S4D/S8; short-dated <6 months excl S4D; vaccines to authorised RN/midwife/paramedic in that pharmacy; urgent use). Records kept 2 years.
- Verbal/phone/email/fax prescriptions: prescriber must send script within 24h; pharmacist has 14 days to receive before notifying NSW Health.
- S8 + S4D AAS: only PAPER scripts stored separately; clear scanned copies allowed.
- Repeats of S8/AAS paper scripts can go back to patient (except OTP).
- S3: adequate verbal/written instructions at supply; no longer must hand personally; pseudoephedrine sale record no longer needed.
- No longer need to record GP when vaccinating; explained S8/S4D losses not reported (but S8 losses recorded in register).
- S8 order receipts can be signed and sent electronically.
- DAA third-party packer must hold TGA GMP licence; supplying pharmacist gives manufacturing instructions; both keep them 2 years. Another pharmacy without GMP packing = it becomes supplying pharmacy.
- Pharmacists can view NSW Health medicine approvals in SafeScript; not expected to confirm approvals.
- Samples of S4D/S8 no longer permitted. Nitrous oxide supply restrictions. Penalties 5 tiers (20–400 pu individuals; 100–2000 pu corporations).
## p108 Pharmacists FAQ (current 20 Nov 2026)
- S3/S4 stored where public has no access (dispensary). S8 (and S4D in some places) per Medicine Storage Standards. Safe keys always held by a pharmacist while open; not left on premises when closed unless locked in another safe; code never disclosed to non-pharmacists. S6 per TGA retail storage guidance.
- S3: supplied by pharmacist with advice/instructions; labelled with pharmacy address. S2/S3 quantity per recognised therapeutic standards.
- Urgent-use supply to practitioner: signed, dated written order (identifies practitioner, medicine/strength/qty, contact details to verify); ARTG-registered; not dispensed in practitioner's name; record e.g. "Prescriber Bag Supply Order"; keep 2 years. No wholesale to shopkeepers/employers.
- Only compound for a particular patient's order/prescription. Never supply expired medicines (even unscheduled).
- Wholesale between pharmacies: same owners or <6-month expiry (S2/S3/S4 not S4D): BOTH record date, name/strength/qty, batch, expiry, supplying & receiving business; 2 years. Pharmacist-to-pharmacist for single patient (incl S8): written signed request, minimum qty, both keep records 2 years. Borrowing stock = this.
- Courier: S8 — receipt from carrier, carrier gets receipt from addressee and returns it; keep consignment + delivery evidence; outer packaging must not show S8/S4D. Australia Post: check their rules.
- S8/AAS repeats with no repeat interval: only original quantity; cancel and retain script. Multiple strengths same S8 on one script OK (not combos). Repeats can go back to patient (not OTP) but copy must be kept every dispensing (photocopy/scan; repeat authorisation alone not enough), 2 years.
- Early S8 supply: prescriber authorisation (verbal confirmed by email/signed), or Section 49 PBS script with 0-day interval, or new script; document reasons and authorisation.
- S4D scripts valid 12 months (also older scripts). Psychostimulant/S8 compounded scripts need no approval number; don't refuse for that. Use SafeScript for decisions.
- Doctor shopping on SafeScript: contact each prescriber; dispense only appropriate quantities; concerns → PSU online enquiry. Make a dated record of prescriber discussions.
- S8 destruction: RCF urgent-use S8 can't be accepted at pharmacy — destroy on RCF premises with DON. Prescriber's S8 may be destroyed by pharmacist, recorded in PRACTITIONER's register, not pharmacy's.
- Records: dispensed scripts kept 2 years from dispensing ON THE PREMISES; scanned copies OK if legible incl. dispensing stickers and producible without delay by pharmacist on duty. Emergency supply records 2 years on premises. S8/AAS copies kept separate from other S4 scripts.
- Electronic drug register: only authorised persons finalise entries (others may make pending entries); responsible person assigns unique credentials incl. to independent witness; deficits reconciled ASAP → if deficit, notify PSU (loss form); surplus → stock count; every adjustment needs a justifiable reason; never write off discrepancies without reason.
- Drug registers: Form h31 from Finsbury Green (or wholesalers/PSA); OTP subsidiary registers from Pharmacy Guild NSW. Part supply: record each supply separately. Register must reflect ALL S8 held incl. patient-held for safekeeping and expired (separate page).
- Interstate prescribers OK if Ahpra-registered; S8: verify independently (contact details from another source); e-scripts need ADHA Conformance ID; OTP prescriber must be OTP-registered.
- Privacy: HRIP Act 2002 HPP 10 limits disclosure. Inspectors (authorised officers) can search/copy records — ask to see photo ID authority card first.
- DOB required on all NSW scripts; if missing, obtain and record in dispensing profile.
- Ownership change: only report if OTP dosing point — email PSU ≥7 working days before takeover. Closing: proprietor stays responsible for records, keep scripts 2 years from last transaction. Emergency closure: staff may under pharmacist direction move scripts (not S8/AAS) etc.
- Prescriber concerns: talk to them, dated record, then PSU/Medical Council/HCCC. Prescriber collecting S8/S4D scripts for family: contact PSU.
- Forged script: verify by phone (number from other source), check lost/stolen list; don't dispense, keep it, notify police; if stolen/illicit form, notify PSU form.
- Addressograph labels only hospital S2-4. Private duplicate script copies destroyed.
- Immunisation: sharps container in immunisation area; licensed clinical waste service.
## Medicine Storage Standards (PDF, Aug 2026, effective 5 Nov 2026) — Part 1 Community pharmacy (Parts 2–3 other settings: n/a)
- Authorised person to access stored S8 in community pharmacy = a pharmacist.
- 1.1 Ambient S8: in a SAFE or dedicated room (strongroom ≥ Grade VII AS/NZS 3809, ≥1 m from external walls), locked when not in immediate use.
  Safe spec: mild steel plate ≥9 mm, continuous welding; flush door ≥9 mm, clearance ≤1.5 mm; fixed locking bar welded inside door near hinge engaging rebate; five-lever lock (or equivalent) fixed to rear face of door.
- Codes/combinations changed at regular intervals; individual codes/biometrics deactivated when person leaves.
- Fastening ("robust fastening mechanisms"): brick/concrete → expanding bolts ≥12 mm x 50 mm; timber → 10 mm coach screws; no fixing points → 10 mm braided steel cable or 5 mm steel chain to loop-end bolt/bar.
- Not accessible to public.
- 1.2 Fridge S8: working fridge locked when not accessed; only S8 + other scheduled substances needing refrigeration; same code rules; robustly fastened; not public accessible.
- All medicines temperature-sensitive: storage areas need adequate climate control.
- Other scheduled medicines may not be stored with S8/S4D (except S9, or fridge as above).
## Electronic Drug Register Standards + Guideline (Aug 2026) — only if the pharmacy uses an e-register
- Mandatory if e-register used (s 109(2)(b)). Entry fields: receipt/supply/administration/loss/destruction/OTP (observed doses, takeaways + days). Paper subsidiary OTP book allowed alongside.
- System must: finalise entries immutably (time, date, user ID, witness ID, ref no.); witness confirmation; error notes; show stock balance after each transaction; reason field for adjustments; reports onscreen/print/CSV for full retention period; admin account with unique logins.
- Guideline (good governance, not mandatory): MFA preferred, no generic accounts, lockout after 5 fails, re-auth after 15 min idle, encryption, audit logs retained same period, blind counts, full export, backups.
- System authorising officer protocol (owner-level oversight): approve user access; unique logins; roles recorded; training; remove leavers; REVIEW ACCOUNTS REGULARLY; REVIEW AUDIT LOGS (by someone who doesn't make entries; at least monthly if high volume); records producible to authorised officer; written notice to Secretary if records/system lost; downtime → compliant bound book, then record balance + fact book used.
- Record name/position of person responsible for the e-register and previous ones, for the retention period.
- APP RULE: the app must never be (or look like) the drug register. It can only hold oversight checks about it.
- S8 orders: ordered & signed by authorised person; phone/email/fax OK but written order to supplier within 14 days; receipt signed & dated, supplier told within 24 h (written/electronic, signed); immediately into safe and recorded in register.
## p036 Supply of prescription medicines (19 Nov 2026)
- S4D/S8 supply record: date, patient, medicine, qty, repeat interval (S8/AAS), dispensing pharmacist; available to authorised officer. Labels per Appendix L.
- Phone (not SMS)/fax/email directions → valid script within 24 h; not received within 14 days → email PSU.
- Emergency 7-day supply (s 33): not S4D or S8. Continued dispensing (Cwlth). NSW Health instruments in emergencies (Orders & Exemptions). Authorities: UTI, skin conditions, OCP resupply (and more expanded scope).
- Urgent use: signed dated order; not routine practice stock; RCF orders from DON/care manager.
- Script validity 12 months (S8: 6 months). Required fields listed. Illegal: pre-populated by others, broker/website/app scripts (non-conformant), images of scripts, paper with digital signature.
## p052 S4D medicines
- S4D monitored in SafeScript: all S4 benzodiazepines, pregabalin, quetiapine, tramadol, zolpidem, zopiclone (+ S8). Storage per Regulation (Storage Standards apply to S4D only in listed places — NOT community pharmacy).
- Pharmacists keep copy of every prescription supplied for 2 years; AAS paper scripts stored separately (except with S8); scans OK; produced without delay.
- Extra care: new patients, unfamiliar prescribers, quantity > PBS limits; check forgery.
- No emergency supply of S4D without prescriber direction. Lost/stolen S4D reported (explained losses not).
## p015 PSU Compliance Policy
- Risk-based: likelihood × consequence matrix → Low/Medium/High/Extreme. Graduated response: education/advice → caution/warning letters → seizure → compliance notices → penalty notices → restrictions/cancellation → prosecution. Referrals: Pharmacy Council (HPCA), HCCC, TGA/PBS, Police.
- Example low risk: inadequate advice on S3 salbutamol. High: high-dose S8 opioids w/o approval.
- APP IDEA: rate open actions with the same likelihood × consequence wording, so the owner fixes high-risk items first.

## p037 OTP legislative requirements / p109 OTP info for pharmacists (Nov 2026)
- Pharmacy dosing point must: hold OTP registration (application to PSU, MOH-Inspectionsandinvestigations@health.nsw.gov.au, ~5 business days); comply with OTP Standards, Medicine Storage Standards, e-register Standards if used.
- NEW registration needed whenever ownership, address or trading name changes (order enough stock first). Eligibility: all owners listed; Pharmacy Council of NSW registration matches; s 90 PBS approval.
- Standard treatment: methadone ≤200 mg/day, buprenorphine ≤32 mg/day; above = prescriber approval. Prescriptions sent directly to dosing point; take-aways dispensed by a pharmacist.
- SafeScript for pharmacists: view approvals, OTP commencement/exit dates, prescriber, dose; confirm or update dosing point details (reduces double dosing). LHD dosing info not in SafeScript.
- NSW Health OTP self-audit tool for community pharmacies (Pharmacy Incentive Scheme incentive for regular self-audits). OTP Clinical Companion; DASAS 1800 023 687; pharmacist advice line 1300 369 772; PSA OTP foundation training; Guild NSW free subsidiary registers.
- OTP meds supplied under s100 HSD Community Access → NSW s100 co-payment program applies.

## NSW Pharmacist Vaccination Standards + Authorities (5 Nov 2026, expire 4 Nov 2028) — pharmacist-new-standard.pdf
- Authority (PH725): pharmacists may administer listed S4 vaccines if APC-accredited training completed; must follow the Standards; s 70 record keeping. Authority (PH720): supply oral cholera/typhoid w/o prescription.
- Approved vaccines + ages: flu 2+; COVID 5+; most 5+; HPV 9+; Shingrix 18+; pneumococcal conjugate 18+; RSV Arexvy 60+ & 50–59 at risk, Abrysvo 60+ and pregnant from 28 wks; JYNNEOS mpox 16+ only in authorised settings; rabies PrEP IM only, non-immunocompromised; oral typhoid 6+.
- Immuniser must: certificate for each vaccine they give; CPR certificate within last 12 months (paediatric CPR if <14 yrs); access AIH, ATAGI, PI, Strive for 5; ANNUAL review of best practice/competence; intern only under direct supervision of certified immuniser. Adopt PSA practice guidelines.
- Equipment at all times: purpose-built vaccine fridge with continuous data logger alarming outside +2 to +8 °C; cold-chain protocol for power outage/fridge failure/outreach; in-date complete anaphylaxis kit with in-date adrenaline; sharps container; displayed emergency response protocol (kit contents, phone access, pre-identified roles); staffing plan for 15-min observation; access to AIH + Strive for 5; process to monitor AIH updates.
- Immunisation room/area: not the dispensary; clean; no patient access to S3/S4/S8, compounding materials or patient records (incl. entry/exit path); auditory + visual privacy; lighting; comfortable temperature; hand washing/sanitiser; impervious bench; 2 chairs (patient chair not stool/wheels); first aid equipment + documented CPR & anaphylaxis procedures; space to lie down; stretcher access. Fridge in public-access room must be locked.
- Storage records: fridge temperature records, storage self-audits, outreach cooler records — per Strive for 5, kept 2 YEARS, given to PHU on request. Breach (outside 2–8 °C or light exposure) of NIP/state vaccines → record + report to PHU 1300 066 055; quarantine at 2–8 °C labelled "Do not use". Private vaccines → manufacturer. Expired NIP/state vaccines discarded and recorded in NSW Vaccine Centre Online Ordering System.
- Each patient: history, contraindications per AIH/PI, refer if contraindicated, informed consent (authority to consent), check expiry, CHECK AIR BEFORE, RECORD ON AIR (preferably 24 h, must be within 10 business days), report pregnancy status.
- Record (cl 48A wording): name, address, DOB, contacts; primary medical practitioner ← NOTE conflicts with p051 "no longer need to record GP" (Standard cites old 2008 Reg) → mark VERIFY; brand, batch, expiry; site; date; immuniser + certificate number; premises; unique reference.
- Consent: written or documented verbal (typed note/checkbox with credentials) for EVERY vaccination; retained 7 years or until 25 if under 18 (HRIP Act).
- Post-vaccination: 15-min observation by immuniser or trained staff; note if left early; aftercare advice.
- AEFI: ambulance for anaphylaxis; notify PHU 1300 066 055 immediately after response, at latest next business day. Administration errors: disclose to patient, record on AIR, report to PHU.

## p062 Vaping (20 Aug 2026) — VERIFIES app's "— verify" vaping steps
- In NSW all vaping goods (nicotine or not) only via pharmacies. Only TGA-notified vapes (TGA List of notified vapes); check stock regularly, remove non-compliant. Mint/tobacco/menthol only; ≤50 mg/mL.
- All unapproved: supply via TGA AP or SAS; search TGA SAS/AP Online System to verify prescription; SAS Category C notification.
- S3 supply (no script): smoking cessation/nicotine dependence; 18+ — REQUEST AND SIGHT ID for identity AND age; ≤1 month's supply, once a month (staged supply allowed); ≤20 mg/mL; tell patient not registered/listed; advice on dose/frequency, length, titration, interactions, alternatives; give quit-service contacts; SAS Category C notification to TGA WITHIN 28 DAYS for each S3 supply.
- S4 supply on prescription (>20 mg/mL or <18): label as dispensed + record. Minors: only prescribed by medical/nurse practitioner with AP/SAS (16+: B or C; ≤15: B).
- Storage: no public access, apart from food; child-resistant packaging (Poisons Standard).
- Only finished products; no component ingredients. Compounding only with AP/SAS script + s 41RC TGA consent + TGO 110 records + sponsor obligations + PBA compounding guidelines.
- Disposal: devices = e-waste (not general/recycling/RUM — battery fire risk); liquids/pods/cartridges OK in RUM bins.
- No advertising of vaping goods to the public.
- Adverse events/faulty goods → TGA AND sponsor within 15 CALENDAR DAYS.
- NSW "Pharmacy self-assessment" tool for vaping storage & supply — use regularly.
## p013 Cannabis medicines (5 Nov 2026) — VERIFIES cannabis steps
- CBD (≥98% CBD) = S4; THC = S8; nabiximols/nabilone/dronabinol S8. Products comply TGO 93; from ODC-licensed manufacturers/wholesalers.
- Dispense unregistered cannabis only where prescriber has TGA AP/SAS approval. Pharmacists may NOT hold stock of unregistered cannabis unless TGA-approved for a particular patient.
- Script must name each active ingredient + strength, dosage, quantity; NO strength ranges or product categories; NO SUBSTITUTION. No emergency supply of unregistered S8 cannabis.
- Must not enter exclusive supply arrangements with a prescriber or third party (Ahpra Code).
- NSW Health approval for S8 to person with substance dependence (prescriber's responsibility).
- S8 storage per Storage Standards; register same day, separate page per brand & strength; loss/theft to NSW Health; destruction by authorised person.
- Driving with THC illegal — inform patients (NSW changes expected late 2026–early 2027).
