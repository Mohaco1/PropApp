# Pharmacy Board of Australia (read 20 Nov 2026)

## Guidelines on the safe provision of pharmacy services including medicines and advice (from 1 Oct 2026)
These replaced the proprietor, dispensing, DAA/staged supply and practice-specific guidelines, which applied until 30 Sep 2026.

**Proprietors:**
- They can't delegate legal or professional responsibilities, wherever they are physically located.
- They must ensure legislation, standards and guidelines are met, and keep a vigilant active interest.
- They must intervene on safety risks.
- All services must be delivered by suitably qualified, legally able people.
- Regulatory action is possible on information from premises regulators.

**Guidance:**
- **1.1** Provide current, fit-for-purpose evidence-based tools, systems and resources.
- **1.2** Systematically assess the risks to safe service delivery (the Board may request evidence of the risk assessment). Specifically:
  - support staff to practise lawfully and confirm their undertaking;
  - balance needs with resources, adding staff when needed;
  - supervise staff appropriately;
  - identify training;
  - make sure pharmacists understand their accountability for support staff;
  - monitor near misses and errors with a systems approach;
  - document and respond to concerns from staff and patients.
- **1.3** All pharmacists report deficiencies to their managers.
- **Patient choice:** no implying an obligation to use a pharmacy through an arrangement; consent to exclusive dispensing can be withdrawn at any time.
- **Privacy:** the APPs, the OAIC Guide to health privacy and the Guide to securing personal information. Staff must understand them; records must be secure.
- Example: suspend a vaccination service when staffing can't support it safely.

## Compounding guidelines (Oct 2024, current), confirmed
- Simple compounding competence comes from the pharmacy qualification.
- Formulas come from reputable references (the APF).
- Sterile or hazardous preparation counts as complex.
- Self-assessment and audit.
- Professional practice profile for complex compounding.
- A risk assessment with clinical justification for EACH request.
- Modifications are recorded in the patient record and annotated on the script.

## CPD
- 40 credits per 12-month period ending 30 September; at least 20 from Group 2/3.
- A CPD plan and records are kept.
