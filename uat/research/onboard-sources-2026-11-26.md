# "Onboard me" spec: sources checked (26 Nov 2026)
Read here (health.nsw.gov.au and ppaonline.com.au are reachable from this environment):
- **NSW OCP initiation protocol (PDF):** a pharmacist may supply the OCP without a prescription if they have obtained "a Graduate Certificate of Advanced Practice and Prescribing for Pharmacists from James Cook University, or; a Graduate Certificate in Pharmacist Prescribing and Advanced Practice from the University of Newcastle, or; a Graduate Certificate in Pharmacist Prescribing from the University of Technology Sydney", called an "approved pharmacist". It also says "the patient must be informed of all costs (including that the OCP will be supplied at private non-PBS cost…)". It doesn't mention the Intention form.
- **NSW Health, Additional services from Queensland-trained pharmacists:** "specified university-level training available through the Queensland University of Technology and James Cook University" covers MSK pain, GORD, rhinitis, acne, and nausea and vomiting. "Queensland-trained pharmacists offering these services must first complete the Intention to provide expanded community pharmacy services in NSW form."
- **NSW OTP Standards (PDF):** "supervised dosing arrangements for 80 or more patients on any given day must implement an…". The Buvidal storage wording is not in the Standards; it's cited to the product information as the brief supplied it.
- **PPA (DAA, Staged Supply, MedsCheck pages):** "From 1 July 2027, all Service Providers participating in the Program must hold current QSPP accreditation" for DAA and Staged Supply. MedsCheck: "From 1 July 2027, Service Providers must hold Quality Safety Pharmacy Program (QSPP) accreditation". Not on the HMR page.
- **NSW Health, Residential care facilities:** the eNRMC "transition period, which ends on 31 March 2027. After this date, only eNRMC systems that fully meet all technical and legislative requirements…".

All other guide steps are worded from the unified spec with their official source links. They're guidance ("walk through", "check"), not new obligations.
