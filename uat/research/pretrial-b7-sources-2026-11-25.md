### NSW Health, Vaccine storage and cold chain management
health.nsw.gov.au/immunisation/Pages/cold-chain-management.aspx
- "Vaccines must be stored in a purpose-built vaccine refrigerator. Domestic refrigerators must not be used for vaccine storage"
- "Purpose-built vaccine refrigerators must be continuously monitored using a data logger"
- "Purpose-built vaccine refrigerator temperatures (current, minimum and maximum) should be checked and recorded manually twice daily. The data logger or thermometer must be reset after each reading."
- "A vaccine storage self-audit must be completed every 12 months and more frequently where there have been problems with: equipment or cold chain breaches."
- "ensuring that stock is rotated when receiving new stock to prevent wastage of vaccines nearing expiry"
- "Before accepting the delivery … check the cold chain monitors and record the check on the vaccine minimum/maximum temperature chart. If the cold chain monitor(s) have activated, contact the NSW Vaccine Centre immediately on 1300 656 132. While awaiting advice, the vaccines must be: placed in the purpose-built vaccine refrigerator, isolated and labelled 'do not use'."
- "outside of the recommended range of +2°C to +8°C during storage or transport. Excursions of >8°C to up to 12°C for no longer than 15 minutes may occur whilst restocking the refrigerator and are acceptable. Immunisation providers must report all cold chain breaches to their local public health unit. Isolate vaccines and place a 'DO NOT USE' sign on the fridge. … If this is not an option, leave vaccines in the affected fridge and keep the fridge door closed. … Do not discard any vaccines. Download and review the data logging report … Do not remove the data logger until the power is resumed (if a power failure)"

## Track C and E
- **Wholesaler invoice spot-check:** Pharmacy Council of NSW, *Supporting resources for managing S8, S4B & S4D medicines in NSW*: "Consider random audits on S8 invoices from wholesalers to ensure stock is accounted for, received, and recorded." Shown as a recommendation.
- **S8 destruction:** NSW Health, Management of Schedule 8 medicines: "Unwanted S8 medicines may be destroyed by a pharmacist who practises at a community pharmacy in the presence of an independent witness." Also: "The destruction must be recorded in the S8 drug register".
- **PBS retention:** National Health (Pharmaceutical Benefits) Regulations 2017 s 59(1) (compilation of 15 Aug 2026): an approved supplier commits an offence if it "does not keep a document required by subsection (3), (4) or (5) that relates to the supply for at least 2 years from the date the pharmaceutical benefit was supplied".
- **PPA retention:** PPA General Terms and Conditions cl 8.2: "You must retain records for the timeframe detailed in the relevant Program Rules". DAA Program Rules s 9: "Service Providers must retain full and true records … for not less than seven years after the claim for payment."
- **Equipment calibration, expiry rotation, IT restore test, shared codes on leaving, complaints themes:** labelled best practice, with no legal claim.

## Track D (built)
- **Pharmacy Council of NSW change of ownership form (March 2020):**
  - "A Notice of Completion must be lodged with Council by the purchasers within 14 days of settlement or transfer of ownership"
  - "Vendors must submit a 'Notice of Disposal of Financial Interest in a Pharmacy Business' within 14 days of selling/transferring their interest."
  - The documents lodged include the "Franchise agreement" and "Finance arrangements – eg. bill of sale, loan offer (loan facility agreement), vendor finance, business security agreement".
- **Mandatory notifications:**
  - Ahpra: "registered health practitioners, employers and education providers must make a mandatory notification in some limited circumstances".
  - Pharmacy Board guidelines: "four concerns that may trigger a mandatory notification … impairment, intoxication while practising, significant departure from accepted professional standards, and sexual misconduct."
- **Privacy Act 1988 s 6D(4)(b):** the small-business exemption doesn't cover an organisation that "provides a health service to another individual and holds any health information".
- **icare:** "When it's time to renew your Workers Compensation policy, you're legally required to declare the amount of wages paid within your business during the previous policy period."
